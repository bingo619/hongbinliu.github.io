ReSkitch Privacy Policy

ReSkitch processes screenshots and annotations on your Mac. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking. The developer does not receive screenshots, project files, shortcut settings or usage data from the app.

Screen access

When you start a capture, ReSkitch uses macOS Screen Recording access to read the screen or window. You can change this permission in System Settings under Privacy & Security. You can open and annotate local images without capturing your screen.

Files and sharing

You choose where to save exported images and editable projects. An editable project includes the original image and its annotations. To share only the visible result of a blur or pixelation, export a flattened image.

Copy places an image on the system clipboard. Dragging an export creates a temporary local file so the receiving app can read it. ReSkitch checks for leftover drag exports when it starts and removes ones older than 24 hours. Any app or service you choose to share with handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Preferences and removal

Capture shortcuts and permission-prompt preferences are stored locally. You can clear shortcuts in ReSkitch Settings and remove saved images or projects in Finder.

Contact

For a privacy question or a request about a support email, contact me@hongbin.info. If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any files you choose to attach and uses them to respond.
